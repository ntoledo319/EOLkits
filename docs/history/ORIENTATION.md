## Reading conventions

The reading path is [`PROJECT_HISTORY.md`](../../PROJECT_HISTORY.md), assembled from the chapters in this directory in the order set by [`.project-history/policy.yml`](../../.project-history/policy.yml): this orientation, the [narrative](NARRATIVE.md), the [ideology](IDEOLOGY.md), the [goals](GOALS.md), the [decision map](DECISION_MAP.md), the [open questions](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), the generated [timeline](TIMELINE.md) and [coverage](COVERAGE.md) indexes, then every event capsule and the contradiction register as appendices.

Four kinds of statement are kept apart: what participants **said** (commit messages, handoffs, PR bodies, ledgers), what the system **did** (code, configuration, workflows at a revision), what **outcome** followed (releases, ledger observations, the absence of revenue), and what the historian **infers**. Claim ids (`CLM-<era>-nnn`) point into [`.project-history/claims.yml`](../../.project-history/claims.yml), where each carries a full commit SHA or another stable locator, an evidence type, a status and a confidence. Principle ids (`P-nn`, versioned as `P-nn.k`) and goal ids (`G-nn`) point into `.project-history/doctrine/`. Contradiction ids (`CON-nnn`) point into the register, which preserves competing accounts with a best-supported reading rather than a verdict. Event ids (`eolkits-YYYY-MM-DD-slug`) are capsules under `.project-history/events/2026/`, one per material decision arc, with occurred/decided/merged/released/recorded dates kept distinct. The initial capsules were backfilled on 2026-09-04; the September 5 workflow event is contemporaneous.

Dates are author dates unless a capsule says otherwise. The era prefixes in claim ids (E1..E4B, EXT) record which reconstruction slice produced a claim and are not themselves era boundaries; the eras are defined in the narrative by evidence.

## Keeping the history current

Commands, from the repository root (also in `policy.yml`):

- `python3 scripts/project_history.py context [paths]` — the smallest relevant orientation, active principles, goals in play and events for the paths you are about to touch.
- `python3 scripts/project_history.py assess [--range A..B] [paths]` — flags potentially material surfaces in new work; advisory, never a verdict.
- `python3 scripts/project_history.py validate` — schemas, ids, dates, cross-links, cited SHAs, secrets, render drift.
- `python3 scripts/project_history.py render` — rebuilds `TIMELINE.md`, `COVERAGE.md`, the decision index and `PROJECT_HISTORY.md` deterministically (byte-stable; no timestamps).
- `python3 scripts/project_history.py audit --full` / `audit --since <anchor>` — compares git and recorded evidence with coverage; detects rewritten ancestry, broken citations, expired deferrals, stale goals, missing outcomes.
- `python3 -m unittest -q tests/test_project_history.py` — the tool's tests.

At task end declare exactly one of `history:recorded <event-id>`, `history:none — <specific reason>`, or `history:defer — <tracking item, owner, deadline>` (the full contract is in `AGENTS.md` §14 and the pull-request template). Record an event when purpose, audience, value, principle, goal or non-goal changed; when a durable API, data-model, architecture, security, privacy, reliability, governance or deployment choice constrains future work; when a release, migration, deprecation, incident, rollback, reversal or external constraint changed direction; when an experiment or failure changed what the project believes; or when a feature or removal changed the user promise. Typos, formatting, generated refreshes, lockfile churn, fixups, mechanical renames and behaviour-preserving refactors are not history. Closed capsules are append-mostly: correct them with a `revision_notes` entry or an amending event, never by rewriting.

CI: `.github/workflows/history.yml` runs validate, the tests, a render-drift check, an incremental audit of the PR range and a secret scan on pull requests with full history, requires one history declaration in the PR body (history-only and dependabot changes exempt), and runs a monthly "history gardener" that writes one drift report.
